This is Sampan

Named for the flat-bottomed boats that carry food, drink and people through the canals and harbours of East and Southeast Asia — Sampan is Oslo's version of the same idea. Kitchen and bar on deck. Music in the hull below.

We're not a fine-dining room. We're closer to the places that stay open late because the night isn't finished yet.

Come for dinner.
Stay for the music.
